System states, screen by screen

One grammar everywhere. Loading = shimmer in the content's own shape. Nothing here yet = dashed, patterned, neutral, with an action. Couldn't load = solid error tint, says what failed, offers Retry. Offline = warning tint plus the data's age. Rate-limited = warning tint plus a countdown. Doesn't apply = plain grey text, no box.
